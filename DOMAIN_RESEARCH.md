# Domain options for a collaborative video editor

Availability snapshot: September 12, 2026, 5:01 a.m. EDT. All names below had no active `.com` registration record in the Verisign registry when checked. Domain status can change at any moment; the checkout screen is final.

## Recommendation

### 1. Cutandem.com

The best one-word brand found. **Cutandem** blends “cut” with “tandem”: people editing the same cut together. It is eight letters, pronounceable, distinctive, and directly tied to both video editing and collaboration. Namecheap showed it as an available, non-premium `.com` at **$11.28 for year one** (or **$6.79** for an eligible new customer using its displayed promotion). Standard renewal is **$18.48 plus the $0.20 ICANN fee**, or **$18.68/year** at today's pricing.

Direct checks: [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/registration/results/?domain=cutandem.com) · [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/cutandem.com)

### 2. Editandem.com

The clearest alternative: “edit in tandem.” It uses the full word “edit,” so its purpose is immediately understandable, but it is one letter longer and slightly less elegant than Cutandem. Namecheap also showed it as an available, non-premium standard-price `.com`.

Direct checks: [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/registration/results/?domain=editandem.com) · [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/editandem.com)

### 3. SharedCut.com

The strongest literal name. It is exactly nine letters, easy to spell, and describes the product in two words: a video cut shared by multiple editors. It is clearer than the coined names, but feels more like a feature name and less like a standalone software brand.

Direct checks: [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/registration/results/?domain=sharedcut.com) · [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/sharedcut.com)

### 4. TakeCrew.com

Eight letters. “Take” gives it a film-production meaning; “crew” makes collaboration obvious. It is warmer and more brandable than SharedCut, although it says production as much as post-production. Namecheap showed the same standard `.com` price.

Direct checks: [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/registration/results/?domain=takecrew.com) · [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/takecrew.com)

### 5. FrameJoin.com

Nine letters and the closest available idea to FrameLine: frames joined into an edit, and editors joining the same workspace. Clear and professional, though slightly more technical-sounding.

Direct checks: [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/registration/results/?domain=framejoin.com) · [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/framejoin.com)

## Names available but best avoided

| Domain | Reason to avoid |
|---|---|
| **Framerge.com** | Excellent concept, but “framerge” is already the name of published Stata software for merging data frames. |
| **ClipPact.com** | An existing video compressor/converter called ClipPact Pro appears in Microsoft Store results. |
| **SpliceMix.com** | Already used for a published machine-learning image method and codebase. |
| **SceneUnit.com** | Already used by a Unity editor utility. |
| **ReelUnit.com** | Already an established Vogelsang railway-service product name. |

These are collision warnings from a preliminary web screen, not a legal trademark opinion. A formal trademark search is worthwhile after choosing two or three finalists and before public launch.

## Registrar choice

**Use Namecheap if the $20 ceiling is firm.** Its published `.com` renewal is $18.48 plus the ICANN fee. It also includes domain privacy.

**GoDaddy misses the ceiling at ordinary list pricing.** GoDaddy's published standard `.com` renewal is $22.99 plus the ICANN fee. Its discounted auto-renew price is listed at $14.99, but relying on a discount program or policy is less predictable than using a registrar whose normal renewal is already under budget. GoDaddy's advertised $0.01 first year also requires a three-year purchase, with later years priced higher.

**Cloudflare Registrar is the lowest-markup alternative.** It charges registry and ICANN cost without markup, but requires using Cloudflare nameservers. It is a good fit if that DNS constraint is acceptable.
